The surroundings

Split — with beaches like Kasjuni and Bačvice — is an easy drive, and the seaside town of Omiš offers a sandy beach at the mouth of the Cetina river plus river rafting. Trogir’s UNESCO old town and Krka National Park both make comfortable day trips.

Korušce and neighbouring Lećevica are inland villages: peaceful, green and far from the summer crowds. The house suits anyone who wants calm as a base and the coast as a day out.
